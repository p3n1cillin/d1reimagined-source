# A Guardian Rises — timeline from public videos (26 October 2026)

The first mission in order, from the end of the opening cinematic to the mission summary,
built only from public console playthroughs (issue #5). It is the visible target for the
stand-in mission script ([`missions/a_guardian_rises.json`](../missions/a_guardian_rises.json));
the object names, objective indexes and subtitle libraries behind each step are in
[the mission walkthrough](mission-a-guardian-rises.md), and the private-reference comparison
is in [the beat-by-beat script](guardian-rises-reference-script.md). This note does not
repeat those; it adds public, linkable timestamps for every step through to the end.

Dialogue is summarised with at most a few quoted words. Times are positions in each video, not
mission clocks: players move at different speeds, so the same beat lands at different times.
Video keys: **A** AyoNiceClips (PS4, launch week, in-game subtitles on), **G** GhostRobo
(PS4, launch day, read from captions only), **R** religiousjedi (2018, post-Taken King),
**M** MKIceAndFire (PS4, launch day, no commentary, two frames). See [Sources](#sources).

On-screen objective titles are drawn in capitals both on the HUD and in Nav Mode, so the
case below is what the screen shows. The library strings behind them are mixed case
("Enter the Wall"; see the walkthrough). Below each title the HUD shows "[touchpad] for
details". Descriptions could be read only in Nav Mode, and only for the first two objectives.

## Version differences to keep in mind

The target is the final PS4 patch (1.33, build 56578). A, G and M are launch-week 2014
footage. R is from December 2018, after The Taken King (September 2015). That update re-voiced
the Ghost (Nolan North replaced Peter Dinklage), so the audio in A, G and M is not the final
audio. The three subtitles read in R ("Hang tight", "Quiet. They're right above us" and "hope
you know how to use that thing") match the launch wording: A's in-game subtitle for the first,
A's and G's captions for the other two. The objective order
and titles are the same in both eras. R's HUD adds a "GHOST" speaker label beside the Ghost
icon. Only the beats with an R time were checked post-Taken King; the rest assume the 2014
order still holds.

## Method

- Transcripts: YouTube's auto-generated captions for A and G, read in the browser. They
  give dialogue order and rough times, but the speech recognition garbles words and mixes
  in the commentators' speech, so wording is checked against in-game subtitles wherever a
  frame shows them.
- Frames: A, R and M were sought to chosen times and paused, and single frames were read
  (720p for A, 1080p for R). This is sampling around each cue, not continuous playback:
  short events between samples may be missed, and enemy counts are estimates from the
  sampled frames and the commentary.
- Nothing was downloaded or kept: no video, frames, audio or game files. Quotes are a few
  words at most; the full subtitle text is in the game's own libraries (see the walkthrough).
- Not covered: the character creation and Traveler intro before the mission, and the Tower
  that follows (see [the reference review](guardian-rises-reference-review.md) for those).
