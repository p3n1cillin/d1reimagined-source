## Orbit and the Director

An idle Guardian in the Tower is returned to orbit ("STILL THERE?", `error.inactivity_timed_out`) within a few minutes. Player input does not prevent it: sense60 was returned to orbit at 22:26 after several minutes of real walking plus a once-a-minute walk and camera turn (sense57: 35 min after arrival; sense60: 15 min). Both times the client sent `peer_leave_request` with reason `0x4ca36d9a`, which the client's error table names `error.inactivity_timed_out`. The CONTINUE departures carry `0x03cfbb27` instead. So it is the client's ordinary player-inactivity timeout, and the input we send does not reset it. Why not is still open. Orbit then shows its announcements, and SET DESTINATION opens the Director's solar-system map. Selecting the Tower there shows only a Taken King quest card ("Hangar Stakes, Requirements Not Met"), and nothing launches.
