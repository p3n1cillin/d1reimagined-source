# PS4 56578: releasing the hidden hangar squads (29 October 2026)

Runs sense52 and sense53 on the owned 56578 image, served from `main`. Private lists stay outside the repository. Host-log times are UTC.

## The problem

Dock 13's "Clear Out the Fallen" encounter has eight squads, `0x062dd95d/1/5..12`. Squads 11 and 12 (`sq_m0_fallen_1/2`) spawned inside an authored region behind level geometry, about (101.1, −580.7, 7.39) with radius 3, and never left it. Their live counts stayed at 1, so the strict eight-squad gate (#63) could never clear; until today the ending used an experimental 5–10 cohort.

## What the client needs

From Codex's static trace of the squad A image:
- `0x1046b0` copies A+0 (an 8-byte object reference) to squad `+0x3e0`, A+0x68 to `+0x3e8` and A+0x6c to `+0x3ec`.
- `0x1047d0` resolves the reference and registers the squad with that objective's task (indexed by A+0x6c) through `0x1c2750`.
- The allocator (`0x171610` / `0x173820`) then picks a subtask and stores its firing-area set at squad `+0x274` and the subtask index at `+0x3f0` (`0x105c50`).
- We served A+0 absent for every Dock squad, so 11 and 12 had no task and no firing area to move to.

The hangar encounter object `obj_m0_hangar_fallen` (`0x062dd95d/3/4`) has nine authored top-level tasks. Task 0 has one subtask, firing-area set `0x062dd95d/42/44`. Its 28-byte A image holds per-task revision counters, not a wave selector.

## The live result (diagnostic)

A list that sets squads 11 and 12's A+0 to the encounter (`5dd92d0603000400`) and A+0x6c to 0, and bumps the encounter's task-0 revision counter A[0] to 1, was published at trigger 41.

- **sense52:** both squads were at their hidden spawn until the list landed (11:19:22). By 11:19:42 they were in the fight room (about (114, −545, 5.1)), roaming x 107–125, y −537 to −549. The test pilot killed them within the next minute.
- **sense53:** with that list as the trigger-41 step and the eight-squad gate restored, the cohort `[5..12]` reached zero at 12:19:05, and the outro and post-game screens followed.

## Caveats

- The task choice (task 0 for both), A+0x68 = 0 and the starting revision counter are inferences. The original mission's own squad-to-task assignment and its timing are not recovered.
- This releases the squads; it does not show that the reference fight releases them at the same moment.
