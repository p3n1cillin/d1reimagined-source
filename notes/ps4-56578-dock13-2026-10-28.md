## Mechanisms established

- **Player triggers are volume-based and need the world clock.**
  - **Arming.** A kind-`0x1d` trigger reports (incident 3403) when the Guardian enters its volume, once armed with A `0x80800463` = `0100000001000000`. Arming trigger 39 at the Divide entry did not fire it until the orange room.
  - **The flood.** Without udt 2 `world_globals_state`, the client's activity tick period is 0 and an armed trigger reports continuously (more than 3,000 reports in 5 minutes).
  - **The fix.** With `D1_AH_WORLD_PERIOD_MS=33.333333` (#61), each arming reports once. Run sense39 recorded exactly one report each for triggers 30, 120, 34 and 35.
- **The host tick must run under load** (#60). Under that report flood no socket read ever timed out, so the control file was never read until the tick also ran after frames.
- **Arrival in slice 104 (bubble 13, Dock 13)** happens in the lamp-lit corridor past the orange rooms, about (132, −567, 6.7), where the "DOCK 13 — OLD RUSSIA" banner shows. The client becomes machine 13. Serving the live list with `@authority 13,2,0` cleared all 22 pending clients, and all 8 Dock squads reported a live count of 1.
- **Dropship pilots.** Activating pilot 7 (drop2) bound an entity, and the exit program advanced its progress word within about 15 s. Afterwards Skiff-turret deaths at the gate stopped dominating. The ship leaving has not been confirmed on screen.
