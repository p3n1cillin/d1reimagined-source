- **Defense 1200 only** (run `character28-defense`, natively delivered: Defense 1200, Light 385, the six body stats 0). The explorer chain plus the combat follower reached the gate in cycle 1. Unlike every earlier run, the Guardian then stayed around the gate for cycles 2–8 instead of dying back to the Divide entry. Point-blank Vandals at the gate doorway (141, −609) still killed it, and the respawn moved to a nearer checkpoint (180.5, −564.3).
- **Defense 1200 plus Attack 200** (run `character29-def-atk`; the combined roster changes only peer bytes `0x840`–`0x845` and `0x8BC`, and both round-trip):
  - The chain reached the exit (153, −604) and a 150 s stand-and-fight saw no nameplates; the shots came from out of view.
  - Stepping through the doorway to (138.7, −607.8) was followed within seconds by a respawn around (166, −590).
  - A later attempt died between the exit and the doorway, and the respawn reset to the Divide entry (298, −654).
- **Where the way on is.** The room past the exit, with the red tarp and the lit doorway (the gate prop, marker 46), is the reference's LEVEL UP room. The doorway is the way on. Straight west of it, lockers block at x ≈ 137, so the route must turn just inside.
- **Reading.** The reference character survives several Vandals here at level 2 with Defense 59. Ours dies in seconds to level-1 Vandals at level 40 with Defense 1200 and Attack 200. A large level advantage should cut incoming damage sharply, so incoming damage is probably not using the level and Defense we serve. The candidates are a missing character stat (the subclass's Armor/Recovery/Agility are 0), the character's progression level, or another input to the damage path. That needs a native trace of incoming-damage scaling before more gate attempts.
- **Tooling.** `launcher.log` (PR #49) worked on its first live use: the replay started without a redirected `boot.log`. The respawn fallback (PR #50) found good copies after deaths: 610, 299, 272.
