# PS4 56578: activity 165 is A Guardian Rises' story cinematic (1 November 2026)

After A Guardian Rises (activity 164), the post-game selection moves to 165, and △ CONTINUE launches it
([29 October](ps4-56578-continue-tower-2026-10-29.md)). Earlier notes and `docs/STATUS.md` called 165
"the next mission". Its world could not be found: the owned record has world tag `ffffffff` and no
video. The run maps 165 to Tower North instead.

## What 165 is

The public world database the project already uses (`manifest/data/world_en.sqlite`,
`DestinyActivityDefinition`) names both entries:

| Index | Hash | Name | Activity type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 164 | `0x6E0DAE89` | A Guardian Rises | 1686739444, `ACTIVITY_TYPE_STORY` (the mission; it carries the reward) |
| 165 | `0x6EAF0959` | A Guardian Rises | 1299744814, `STORY_CINEMATIC` |
| 166 | `0xB9FCCAC3` | Restoration | `ACTIVITY_TYPE_STORY` (the next mission) |

The [14 October content table](ps4-56578-blockers-2026-10-14.md) already listed A Guardian Rises as
"164/165". So 165 is the cinematic that follows the mission, with no world of its own and no
objectives. It plays and then puts the Guardian in the Tower. That is why its record has no world
tag, and the run's mapping (165 → Tower North) is the right destination. Restoration (166) is the
next mission.

## What this changes

- **No longer open:** 165's own world or scripted steps.
- **Open instead:** the cinematic's video, which the run does not play before Tower North. The
  owned record shows no video either, so where the client finds it is the next question.
- The world table rows `0` and `165` in `ps4/fixtures/56578/mission/world-table-tower.txt` stay as
  they are.
