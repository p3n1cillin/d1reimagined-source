# PS4 56578: the Ghost over the palm (F54), what 12 November ruled out (12 November 2026)

This continues the [8 November note](ps4-56578-ghost-option-2026-11-08.md). The fault has two parts.
Outdoors, after the resurrection, the open hand with the Ghost over it stays in first person until
the first weapon. In the original it is only there for the scripted moment before control. In the
dark Wall, the player's own Ghost sits centre-screen with scanner rings. In the original it is not
drawn. Neither part is fixed. This note records what was learned and ruled out. It also corrects
two statements: one in the 8 November note and one in the host's code comment.

## Reference

- A public 2014 PS4 recording of the mission (a new Warlock) was compared frame by frame with our
  recordings.
- Original: hand and Ghost only before control. No hands afterwards.
- Original, inside the Wall: no Ghost model, and a blue light.
- A Guardian made through character creation on our servers shows the same fault as the lab's test
  character.

## What the client's fields say (in game, read from memory)

- The object the Ghost-in-hand code works on is reached from the player entry at `+0x2708`. Its own
  handle is at `+0x24`. It is not the unit at `+0x644`.
- On that object:
  - `+0x1224`, `+0x1228` and `+0x122c` are the Ghost references (-1 when empty).
  - `+0x140c` is a timestamp (-1 when none).
  - `+0x11f8` is the owning player's handle.
  - `+0x141b` holds flags: `0x08` gear applied; `0x02` inside a Ghost light volume. `0x741690`
    recomputes the flags each tick.
  - `+0x1418` is the touchpad summon flag.
- Runs gh42 and gh45: outdoors, with the hand on screen, the Ghost references are -1, `+0x1418` is
  0 and the flags are `0x08`.
- Inside the Wall, the references are set, flag `0x02` is on, and the Ghost object's state byte
  `+0x2a8` is 9.
- Within two seconds of the rifle pickup, the references are -1 again. The Ghost object goes through
  state 10.
- So the outdoor hand is not the Ghost-in-hand state that `0x742820` / `ghost_deploy` /
  `ghost_summon` manage.
- The in-Wall Ghost is the light-volume Ghost (state 9). It is dismissed when the volume's
  condition ends.
- The global at `0x25D7274` holds `0x0150F05D`. This is confirmed as the option that forces the
  light-volume flag off.
- That option is in no live capture. So the original did not use it for this mission.

## Decompile notes (build 56578)

- Ghost state byte `+0x2a8`, values and writers:
  - 1: in hand (`0x6470d0`).
  - 2 to 8: intermediate.
  - 9: light-volume Ghost (`0x6478d0`).
  - 10: dismissing (`0x6483b0`).
  - 0: none.
- Action classes:
  - `ghost_deploy`: start `0x379980`, end `0x379c80`.
  - `ghost_summon`: start `0x37bdb0`, update `0x37c050`, end `0x37bf10`.
  - `action_impl_resurrect`: start `0x38cb50`, end `0x38cc80`, update `0x38cd20`. It plays an
    animation on a component. It runs until the animation finishes or the owner entity's `+0x44`
    stops being -1.
- In game, entity `+0x44` stayed -1 across the rifle pickup (gh45). So that end condition is not
  what the pickup changes.

## Corrections

- The 8 November note, and the comment on `D1_AH_UNARMED_UNTIL_PHASE`, say the live host kept the
  player bundle's `+8` and `+9` at 0 until the rifle. They also say it set them "three seconds after
  the rifle's checkpoint (527 s)". That is wrong.
- Live capture (build 51539), decoded against the 56578 bundle layout with the one leaf 51539 lacks
  (the i32 at `+0x38`) inserted:
  - `+8` is set at 373.0 s.
  - `+9` is set at 374.0 s.
  - Both are around the first cinematic's start.
- 527 s is a relist four seconds after the first-person cinematic stopped.
- The weapon incident is at 618.6 s.
- Other bundle leaves that the live host set and ours does not:
  - `+0` (the player's bubble): 14, later 3, 12, 13. Ours is -1.
  - `+0xB` = 3.
  - `+0xC` = 1.
  - `+0x34` = 1.0.
  - `+0x48` = 1.
  - `+0x72` = 0xBC00.
  - `+0x3D` is 1 from 528.8 s (0.8 s after the spawn) until 619.8 s (1.2 s after the weapon
    incident).
- The RTTI does not name the meanings of these leaves.

## Ruled out (adds to the 8 November list)

- The first-person cinematic not ending.
- The cinematic rows' per-viewer fields. The live host also sent no object and no filter.
- The slot handover answers.
- The bundle's `+8` / `+9` timing.
- The other bundle leaves, as served at list load.
- Entity `+0x44`.
- A late or missing hop-on filter as the cause of the in-Wall Ghost.
