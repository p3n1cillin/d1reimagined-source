# PS4 56578: the Ghost over the palm (F54), what 12 November ruled out (12 November 2026)

This continues the [8 November note](ps4-56578-ghost-option-2026-11-08.md). The fault has two parts.
Outdoors, after the resurrection, the open hand with the Ghost over it stays in first person until
the first weapon. In the original it is only there for the scripted moment before control. In the
dark Wall, the player's own Ghost sits centre-screen with scanner rings. In the original it is not
drawn. Neither part is fixed. This note records what was learned and ruled out. It also corrects
two statements: one in the 8 November note and one in the host's code comment.

## Reference

- A public 2014 PS4 recording of the mission (a new Warlock) was compared with our recordings at
  steps of three to four seconds.
- Original, before control: hand and Ghost.
- Original, after control starts: no hands. The Ghost is seen flying ahead, then not drawn.
- Original, inside the Wall: no Ghost model. A light flare, then darkness.

## Read from memory (in game)

Field values and their changes, from the lab runs. Nothing here is a reading of the code.

- Outdoors (the hand was seen on screen in gh42 and gh46; gh45 was not recorded with the hand): the
  Ghost references are -1, `+0x1418` is 0, and the flags are `0x08`.
- Inside the Wall: the references are set, flag `0x02` is on, and the Ghost object's state byte
  `+0x2a8` is 9.
- The references were -1 again by the next five-second sample after the rifle pickup (gh42, gh45).
  State 10 was sampled once, in gh46, just before the references cleared.
- Global, read from memory in gh45 and gh46: the global at `0x25D7274` holds `0x0150F05D`.
- Own runs: a Guardian made through character creation (run new8) shows the same outdoor hand with
  the Ghost. That run ended before the Wall, so the in-Wall part was not seen for a created
  Guardian.

## Readings from the decompile (build 56578)

What each field is taken to be. These are readings, not confirmed facts.

- Fields on the object reached from the player entry at `+0x2708` (its own handle is at `+0x24`;
  it is not the unit at `+0x644`):
  - `+0x1224`, `+0x1228`, `+0x122c`: the Ghost references (-1 when empty).
  - `+0x140c`: a timestamp (from the timer code at `0x741e60`; -1 when none).
  - `+0x11f8`: the owning player's handle.
  - `+0x141b` bit `0x08`: gear applied. Bit `0x02`: inside a Ghost light volume. This is an
    inference from the structure of `0x741690`, which recomputes the flags each tick.
  - `+0x1418`: a per-local-player interface flag fed by the touchpad state (per the 8 November
    note).
- Option `0x0150F05D`: by the reading of `0x741690`, an option with that hash, if present and true,
  forces the light-volume flag off.
- The option was not found in the live captures. Those captures cannot show its absence (the
  8 November note explains why). So they do not show whether the original used it.
- This reads as: the outdoor hand is not the Ghost-in-hand state that `0x742820` / `ghost_deploy` /
  `ghost_summon` manage.
- This reads as: the in-Wall Ghost is the light-volume Ghost (state 9), dismissed when the volume's
  condition ends.

## Decompile notes (build 56578)

- Ghost state byte `+0x2a8`, values and writers:
  - 1: in hand (`0x6470d0`).
  - 2 to 8: intermediate.
  - 9: light-volume Ghost (`0x6478d0`).
  - 10: dismissing (`0x6483b0`).
  - 0: none.
- Action classes:
  - `ghost_deploy`: start `0x379980`, end `0x379c80`.
  - `ghost_summon`: start `0x37bdb0`, update `0x37c050`, end `0x37bf10`.
  - `action_impl_resurrect`: start `0x38cb50`, end `0x38cc80`, update `0x38cd20`. It plays an
    animation on a component. It runs until the animation finishes or the owner entity's `+0x44`
    stops being -1.
- In game, entity `+0x44` stayed -1 across the rifle pickup (gh45). So that end condition is not
  what the pickup changes.

## Corrections

- The 8 November note says the live host served the player bundle's `+8` and `+9` at 0 until the
  rifle, then 1.
- The comment on `D1_AH_UNARMED_UNTIL_PHASE` says the live host set them "three seconds after the
  rifle's checkpoint (527 s)". That timing is wrong.
- Live capture (build 51539) is an Xbox 360 capture. Its bundle bits were decoded against the 56578
  bundle layout, with the one leaf 51539 lacks (the i32 at `+0x38`) inserted. This consumed exactly
  273 bits in all 195 updates of that row. That is the fit evidence. It still does not prove that
  the leaves mean the same thing.
- On that capture, `+8` was set at 373.0 s and `+9` at 374.0 s, around the first cinematic's start.
- 527 s is a relist four seconds after the first-person cinematic stopped.
- The weapon incident is at 618.6 s.
- Other bundle leaves the live host set and ours does not:
  - `+0` (the player's bubble): 14, later 3, 12, 13. Ours is -1.
  - `+0xB` = 3.
  - `+0xC` = 1.
  - `+0x34` = 1.0.
  - `+0x48` = 1.
  - `+0x72` = 0xBC00. It was already 0xBC00 in the live list at 371.7 s, so it was not set later.
  - `+0x3D` is 1 from 528.8 s (0.8 s after the spawn) until 619.8 s (1.2 s after the weapon
    incident).
- The RTTI does not name the meanings of these leaves.
- This note does not change the comment on `D1_AH_UNARMED_UNTIL_PHASE`, or the profile's `164:3`.

## Ruled out (adds to the 8 November list)

- The first-person cinematic not ending.
- The cinematic rows' per-viewer fields. The live host also sent no object and no filter.
- The slot handover answers.
- `+8` / `+9` armed 32 s before the spawn (gh46) and armed from the start (gh47). Arming at the
  live host's exact time was not tested.
- The other bundle leaves, as served at list load.
- Entity `+0x44`.
