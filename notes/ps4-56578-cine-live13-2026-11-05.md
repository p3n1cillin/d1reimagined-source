**Result:** activity 165, `cine_reveal`, the story cinematic that CONTINUE launches after A Guardian Rises, played start to finish from our own servers, and then the activity ended.

- **Seen:** the screen frames show the Guardian's jumpship in flight, then the reveal shot of the Traveler over the Last City and the Tower, in cinematic letterbox.
- **The client's own reports:** incident 2681 (cinematic started) and 891 (stopped).

It is the first cinematic to play on our servers. The one earlier run that reached 165, cine-live8, served lifetime phase 3 throughout, and the client never started a cinematic.

## What made the difference

The live hosts stepped the world's lifetime record (`0x4786C0E0`/`0x10`/3) in this order:
1. 0;
2. 1;
3. 4, and the cinematic played two ticks later;
4. 6 when it stopped;
5. 8.

That is the order in both cinematic captures (`d1-live-speaker-timeline-2026-11-05.md`). The fixtures now carry it (#292, and the `*-end.txt` lists here). `d1-cine-watch.py` serves it in order.

The served host was unchanged: the private 2 November build, with stamp 0 and a fresh full frame every 10 s. So the 10 Hz stamps and deltas (#291, #295) were **not needed for the start**.
