# PS4 56578: orbit and the Director, driven by pad (7 November 2026)

On 7 November the lab bot reached the Director and launched a story mission from it, as a player
would. That was runs spk5 (after The Speaker cinematic) and ven1 (a geared character booted straight
to orbit). This note records how to drive it and what the Director refuses.

## Driving it

The keys are the lab's keyboard pad map (`tools/ps4_setup/d1_keys.py`):

| Key | Pad |
| --- | --- |
| NumPad2 | Cross |
| NumPad6 | Circle |
| W, A, S, D | left stick |
| Space | the touchpad |

1. **The notices.** The first orbit shows one notice after another. Dismiss each with Cross:
   - "New in Rise of Iron" (a geared character);
   - Shaxx's "Crucible unlocked";
   - Armsday;
   - "Age of Triumph: exclusive for Rise of Iron owners" (a short "Please Wait…" before its OK);
   - "Venus: Ishtar Sink, destination revealed".

   **The Destiny 2 pre-order panel** also appears. Its Pre-order buttons sit under the cursor, so
   dismiss it with Circle, not Cross.
2. **Set Destination.** It is an on-screen button at the bottom left, not a hotkey. The circle in the
   middle of the screen is a cursor on the left stick, which moves about 1 px per ms of hold at full
   resolution. From the centre, the button is reached by A 900 ms + S 500 ms, then D 280 ms + W 180 ms;
   then press Cross.
3. **The Director.** Its Solar System page shows Venus, the Reef, the Moon, Earth, the Tower, Mars and
   Saturn, plus Vanguard and Crucible. It opens with no cursor shown; a short stick nudge shows it.
   Cross on a planet opens that planet's map, and the map scrolls with the cursor. Hovering a node
   shows its card, and Cross on a launchable one sets it as the destination.
4. **Launch.** Orbit then shows the activity's name with a green LAUNCH button at the bottom right.
   Move the cursor there and press Cross: "Launching in 0:00".

Every step was checked against the emulator's own screenshots (`lab/tower-route/fight.py` `grab`).

## What it offers and refuses (current fixtures)

| Node | Card | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earth: The Dark Within | Level 3 story, Resume or Restart | **Launched.** About 24 s of black, then in world at Devil's Spire (spk5). |
| Earth: The Silent Fang | House of Wolves, Level 18 story, **Requires Better Gear** | Cross does nothing. |
| Venus: A Stranger's Call | Level 10 story, **Requires Better Gear** | Cross does nothing. The rest of Venus is padlocked. |
| Tower: Hangar Stakes | The Taken King cinematic, **Requirements Not Met** | Cross does nothing. |

**F58, "Requires Better Gear".** It shows with a level-40, Light-385 character (ven1) on missions
whose unlock rules pass. In `ps4/fixtures/56578/characters/activity-rules.tsv`, 168/169, 184/185,
220/221 and 614/616 all need 2668 or 2670, or 419 or 440, and all four are served. So the gate is
something else. One suspect is a power value the client works out from the equipped items' own
stats instead of the profile's Light override; our served items don't carry those stats. The text
is in neither the executable nor the public manifest, so it comes from the client's string tables.

## The loading screen

**F48, the ship flight.** For the Director launch, the client logged `Activity is NOT matchmade --
using the cinematic UI`, so it chose the flight sequence. The screen was still black for about 24 s.
There were no AvPlayer or Bink calls, so the flight is drawn in-engine. Launches straight from
character select log `loading cinematics are suppressed -- using the common loading UI` instead;
see `lab/tower-route/probe-cine-suppress-readonly.py` and
[The Speaker note](ps4-56578-speaker-2026-11-07.md).
