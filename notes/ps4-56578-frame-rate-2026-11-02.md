# PS4 56578: frame-rate presets (2 November 2026)

The goal (the user's, 2 Nov): frame-rate presets (30, 60, 100, 120, 144, 165, 240, uncapped) and any
resolution up to 4K, ending as options in the game's own settings menu. This note covers the frame
rate's first step: fixed presets that work in game.

## How the game paces frames

- The 60 Hz patch (`ps4/patches/CUSA00219-56578-lab-orbit-60hz.xml`) sets the engine's time control
  at `0x592A03..0x592A21`: period 1001/60 ms, rate 60, step 1000/60 ms.
- Read live with `tools/ps4_setup/d1-frame-timing.py`, the presentation cadence table (behind the
  pointer at `0x26BCD08`) is built from them. `+0x38` is the rate and `+0x08` the period in
  microseconds, and the engine's game-time clock (`*(0x26BC880)`) ticks at the rate.
- The table's `+0x28` stays 16,683 us at every rate. The 60 Hz patch's "outer cadence" entry points the
  present deadline at it (`0x594A3B`). That is the same as the original `+0x08` read at 60 and a
  60 fps cap at any other rate, so above 60 it must be off.

## Presets

`tools/ps4_setup/d1-fps-patch.py N` writes the patch for rate N from the 60 Hz one. It sets period
1001/N ms, rate N and step 1000/N ms, and disables the outer cadence entry for N != 60; at 60 the
output is byte-identical to the original. The emulator must also present that fast:
`GPU.vblank_frequency >= N` in `user/config.json` (edited byte-exact) and `D1_FLIP_RATE=0`.

Measured in orbit (lab session, geared Guardian, emulator FPS counter on):

| Preset | Table period | Engine | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 16,683 us | 60.0 ticks/s | 60 FPS (16.7 ms) |
| 120 | 8,341 us | 120.0 ticks/s | 120 FPS (8.3 ms) |
| 240 | 4,170 us | 239.9 ticks/s | 194 FPS (5.1 ms), rendering-bound on this PC |

The engine's period matches its tick rate at each preset (8.3 ms × 120), so game time runs at real
speed.

## In the mission, and switching live

In the mission's opening at 120 (`d1-agr-run.ps1` with the 120 Hz patch, vblank 120), the screen
showed about 41 FPS (this PC) and the engine ticked 80-85 times a second: **about 0.7x real
time**, and the loading sat much longer than at 60.
- The engine takes at most two ticks per frame. At rate N, game time is real only while the screen
  holds N/2 FPS; at 60 that is 30, which this PC manages in the world.
- A preset above what the PC can render is slow motion, not smoother. The settings need a guard:
  the rate must follow what the screen can hold.

**The rate can be changed while the game runs.** Its live state is three values:
- the cadence table's `+0x08` (period in us, 1001000/N) and `+0x38` (rate);
- the clock's `+0x08` (tick in s, 1/N).

`tools/ps4_setup/d1-frame-rate.py N` writes them and measures the result. In the running mission it
switched 120 → 60 (60.0 ticks/s, 1.0x; the stalled loading screen then came through), 60 → 120
(85 ticks/s, 0.7x) and back (60.3, 1.0x), with no restart. That is the mechanism the settings menu
needs. A limiter can use it too, lowering the rate to what the screen can hold.
