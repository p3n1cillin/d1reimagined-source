servers in run awk5. It is the third cinematic activity to do so, after the reveal and The Speaker.

   set 17 and `/6/1` never started.

**Step 4's wait.** The host never logged the client entering slice set 1: its udt-21 tracker
(`track_current_slice`) logged no slice set in this world, in awk3 or awk5. In A Guardian Rises it
logs the walk into 24. So the slice gate waited its full fallback, which was 900 s then. The client's
own log had finished the teleport 30 s after 891, and `/6/1` started 13 s after its play list. The
schedule now waits for slice set 1 or 30 s. Live, `/6/1` played 83 s after `/6/0`, about 11 s after a
72 s first cinematic. Why the tracker misses this world's slice sets is still open.
