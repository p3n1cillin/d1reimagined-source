## ✅ Black, blue-tinted shadows: the colour-grading LUT lost its dark slices (runs `2026-10-26-gfx1`–`gfx18`)
- Symptom: everything in shadow came out pure black (RGB ≈ 0) with saturated blue patches, while sunlit surfaces looked roughly right. On the highway ~10% of the frame was black.
- New diagnostics in the D1 shadPS4 build, all off unless their variable is set:
  - `D1_DUMP_DIR=<dir>`: touch `<dir>.flag` and, for the next 0.2 s, every texture a shader reads (first time per address and shader) is copied out linear as `SEQ_ADDR_SHADER_WxH_FORMAT_BPP.raw` (3D textures: all slices stacked), plus `events.txt` (every draw's textures `T`, colour targets `RT` with slice range and footprint, depth target `DB`, in order). `tools/ps4_setup/d1-dumpconv.py DIR` turns a dump into PNGs and per-channel statistics. This replaces a graphics debugger.
  - `D1_TEX_SURVEY=1`: logs each shader's distinct textures (type, size, format, tiling, swizzle) and each pixel shader's render targets once.
  - `D1_TRACE_IMAGE=<hex>`: traces lookups, creation, expansion and freeing of the images at that address, while a dump is capturing (interleaved with that address's draw events).
  - `D1_META_RUN=1`: run compute writes to addresses still registered as HTILE/CMASK metadata instead of skipping them (no such writes on the highway; kept as a lever).
- What the dumps showed: the G-buffer (albedo sRGB + AO, normals, depth/stencil), the screen-space sun shadow mask and the sky/fog buffer are all correct, and the deferred composite (`0x90e5e10567631e04`) gives shadowed pixels 1/20–1/400 of the sunlit value, so the HDR image was fine. The 32³ grading LUT the tonemap (`0x27d596fb07cd3321`) samples at `0x377B10000` was not: slice 0 mapped every red value to the same dull blue (0.02, 0.08, 0.44).
- Why: the game renders the LUT one slice per draw (`0xf26f085511bab1f0`, a 2D array of z+1 layers, view slice z) from a 1024×32 strip, then samples it as 3D. Its memory is a pooled allocation that two other targets reuse in the same frame: a 32×32 RGBA8 target (`0x44734111020c5924`, one layer but an 8-layer footprint, `0x8000` bytes) drawn before it, and a 12×18 UI target after. At slice 7 the 8-layer request "perfectly matched" the other 32×32 image (same size, format and byte size), so the cache expanded *that* image. From then on two chains grew side by side, and the overlap loop kept whichever it visited last: the wrong one, whose slices 0–6 were the other target's output and stale memory. Slices 0–6 are the low-blue end of the LUT, which is why dark colours went black or blue.
- Fix (`tools/ps4_setup/shadps4-0.19.0-texture-cache-d1.patch`, also in the full `shadps4-0.19.0-local-ip.patch`): a render target that grows a 2D array by one layer extends its **predecessor**: same format and size, exactly one layer fewer, and a footprint of exactly those layers. The cache drops the other images at that address. Together with the earlier "a slice target never writes through a view of the 3D volume" rule (now the default; `D1_VOLUME_SLICES=0` restores upstream), the LUT builds as one chain every frame. The dumped LUT now maps black to black and white to white, with smooth ramps between.
- Result on the highway (same spot): black 9.8% → 0.0–0.1%, mean 139 → 152. Shadows show ambient sky light and texture detail, the gun is grey instead of black and blue, and the dark tunnels before The Breach are dim but readable.
- The lit Breach (run `2026-10-26-gfx18`, near the rifle): the pipes that rendered blown-out white in `agr-breach22` now show warm light and rust. The wrong LUT chain had also broken its bright end.
