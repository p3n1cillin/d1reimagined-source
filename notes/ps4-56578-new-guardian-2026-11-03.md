# PS4 56578: booting a new Guardian (3 November 2026)

Every profile the lab serves carries a later save's completion record (field [11]: 94 subs at boot,
95 in the grant and post-game pushes). A new Guardian has none of them. #228 added
`tools/ps4_setup/d1-new-guardian-profiles.py` (the five served profiles with the record cleared, plus
the subs the mission itself grants) and `ending-run.ps1 -PostgameDir`, untested in a run: "the 94
subs may hold something the route relies on".

## The first run with it

Session newguardian-run1: `ending-run.ps1 -Waves -PostgameDir <new-guardian>\postgame -ExtraEnv
"D1_PROFILE_FILE=<new-guardian>\characters\profile-tutorial-t.bin,D1_LOOT_GRANT=0x827dbaf0=0xd100000000000401:<fixtures>\characters\grants\khvostov-3598.bin|<new-guardian>\grants\profile-tutorial-th.bin"`.

- The prologue played and was skipped as usual. Then, instead of the mission, the client showed
  three screens in turn, each waiting for a button:
  1. **NEW IN RISE OF IRON** (Light 385, the raid, Crucible updates, the campaign) - OK;
  2. **AGE OF TRIUMPH** ("exclusive for Rise of Iron owners") - OK;
  3. **DESTINY 2, September: pre-order now** - Dismiss (circle).
- After them it stood in **orbit**, with "VENUS: ISHTAR SINK, DESTINATION REVEALED" (from the served
  unlock flags, which the run does not change). A Guardian Rises did not start.
- The launcher had timed out meanwhile ("TIMEOUT waiting for Entering state 'activity:in_world'",
  then "... transitioning:teleportation"), so the replay scanned an orbit screen and tracked nothing.
  The run was stopped there; the route itself was not exercised.

## What it means

- **The old record hid real boot-flow behaviour.** The announcement screens are marked seen by subs
  of the completion record; our lab profiles had them, so no run ever saw the screens. A 2017 retail
  account opening the game for the first time would have.
- **The mission's start depends on more than the active activity.** With the announcements up during
  the prologue, the client ended in orbit, not in A Guardian Rises. Whether it would have entered the
  mission had the screens been dismissed in time (before the launcher's wait ran out), or whether a sub
  of the record is what sends a new character into it, is open.

## Bisecting the record (orbit boots)

`tools/ps4_setup/d1-boot-screens.py` records what an orbit boot (`d1-lab-served.ps1 -Profile`) shows
before the player can act: it presses cross at the title, then saves each screen and dismisses it
(cross, or circle when cross does nothing). Each profile is the stock boot profile's record with a
subset of its 94 subs kept (`d1-profile-completions.py --clear --set ...`).

| Round | Subs kept | What the boot showed |
|---|---|---|
| r1 | the first 46 (23-547: story completions and nearby) | orbit, then CRUCIBLE UNLOCKED, ARMSDAY, EVERVERSE GRAND OPENING, NEW IN RISE OF IRON, AGE OF TRIUMPH (then, probably, the Destiny 2 screen) and VENUS REVEALED |
| r2 | the other 48 (673-2188) | **no orbit**: A Guardian Rises' opening cinematic (the Ghost's search, the Fallen), as for a new character |

So the subs that mark the announcements seen are among 673-2188, and the first half (the story
completions) decides between orbit and the new-player start. A new Guardian (no subs) gets both: the
announcement queue came up during the prologue in newguardian-run1 and the client ended in orbit.
